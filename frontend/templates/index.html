Common Questions

Is my data secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption to protect all resident and financial information.

Can I pay maintenance via credit card?

Absolutely. Our Stripe integration supports credit cards, debit cards, and net banking.

How do I add a family member to my unit?

As a primary resident, you can easily add family members from your dashboard under the "My Profile" tab.

What happens if I forget my password?

You can use the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page to securely reset your password via email.

Can I book amenities in advance?

Yes, you can pre-book amenities up to two weeks in advance, depending on your society's specific rules.
